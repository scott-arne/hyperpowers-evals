Where should the filters go on the account activity page?

Wireframes use the real page content. The controls shown here are placeholders: which filters to include is the next question. In every option a "Showing X of 48 events" line is read out to screen readers when the results change.

Filter bar above the table

The controls are always visible between the intro and the table. Easiest to discover and the simplest to build. It takes up a row of vertical space.
