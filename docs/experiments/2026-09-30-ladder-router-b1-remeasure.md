# 2026-09-30: the bootstrap ladder on router brief b1, twenty sessions per arm

**Hypothesis.** Pre-registered at `dc6a8f7` before any session launched. The adoption-remediation campaign (2026-09-23) passed router brief `brainstorming-router-escalates-b1-userid-param` 1 of 3 on the bootstrap-ladder tree; this campaign asks whether the ladder lowers b1's pass rate or whether that miss was a small-sample draw. A session passes when its composed final verdict is pass. The bar is treatment at least 14 of 20; a regression is treatment below control with a one-sided Fisher exact p < 0.05, read as "ladder rung 1 is revised or the ladder reverts, the human partner's call".

**Verdict.** Regression: the pre-registered negative. Control passed 16 of 20 and treatment 6 of 20 against a bar of 14; one-sided Fisher p = 0.0018. Treatment's other 14 are 12 fails and 2 sessions indeterminate after their one re-run, which count as not passing; control's other 4 are fails. The direction does not rest on the grader: every counted fail in both arms also fails the scenario's deterministic post-check (`find` for a spec under `docs/*/specs/`), so treatment wrote a spec in 8 counted sessions and control in 16, and counting treatment's two indeterminates as passes gives 8 of 20 and p = 0.0112, still a regression. The Phase 3 miss was not a draw. Diagnostics, read from the 40 manifest-row transcripts after the tally and not scored: treatment opened with a bounded classification in 20 of 20 sessions, each citing the entry point ("one function, one caller, one file"); control opened architectural or explicitly not bounded in 9, plainly bounded in 8, and hedged in 3. In the text up to that first classification, control used "outcome" in 7 of 20 against treatment's 0, and named new structure ("new module", "subsystem", "doesn't have") in 15 against 5; the interface-or-signature cue sat at ceiling in both (18 and 20), and 2 treatment sessions named the ladder. So the suspected mechanism, rung 1's "an interface others call" turning the brief into a confirm followed by bounded, does not fit. What fits is that treatment sizes the request by its edit rather than by the outcome it names ("so we can track who logged in", identity and tracking the repository lacks), which is the thought brainstorming's own red flag answers: "The code I'd touch is right here, so it's bounded".

**Limits.** One brief, one model, one Claude Code version; whether other briefs whose outcome names missing structure regress the same way is unmeasured. The control is `main`, not the branch minus the ladder; the other differences a routing session loads are the `Assumption:` bullet, which acts after classification, and the `optimizing-performance` quoting fix. 11 sessions came back indeterminate, 9 originals and 2 of their re-runs, every one a Gauntlet-Agent `investigate` on a completed session that opened bounded and upgraded after the scripted clarification; the grader's reading of that late upgrade is exactly where the composed verdict is least certain, which is why the post-check corroboration and the sensitivity count above matter. The follow-ups ran at lower concurrency than the main batch. Two grader voids, both socket closures and one coinciding with a host network disconnect, used 2 of control's cap of 3; both are archived without a `result.json`. The mechanism reading is an interpretation of transcripts, and the cue patterns are crude.
