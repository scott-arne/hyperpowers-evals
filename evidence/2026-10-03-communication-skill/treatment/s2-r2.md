Fix the ordering, and start it today. 1.5 days with the staging soak lands Thursday, which leaves about half a day before Friday's release. If the soak is not clean by Thursday evening, Friday ships without the billing change and the fix goes in the next release. Nothing else in the release touches billing, so the four announced changes do not depend on it, and the cost of waiting is 10 minutes of support time per flagged order while reconciliation keeps catching them.

I would not do the retry, for two reasons. The 500 clean local runs show that a local commit finishes inside 200 ms. We have not measured commit latency in production, and the nightly batch is the likeliest place for it to pass 200 ms, so the flags would come back there at a lower rate. And once the flags mostly stop, nobody will schedule the ordering fix, and we keep the race with a 200 ms sleep in the checkout path. The retry reaches the same three callers as the fix, so it is only smaller in lines changed.
