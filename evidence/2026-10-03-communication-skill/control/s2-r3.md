Neither one goes in Friday. Ship the four announced changes with billing untouched, and do the ordering fix properly next week with a real soak.

Why:

- Nothing else in the release touches billing, so there is no coupling that forces a billing change into Friday. That's the fact that decides it. The question isn't retry vs. proper fix for Friday; it's whether billing needs to change this week at all, and it doesn't.
- The cost of waiting is small and bounded. Reconciliation has caught every case, no money has moved wrong, and each flag is ten minutes of support time. A few more days of that is cheap.
- The retry is a timing fix for an ordering bug. 500 local runs at 200 ms tells you nothing about production load or the nightly batch. And it doesn't buy safety, since reconciliation already contains the damage; it buys fewer flags, which mostly means the race gets quieter and the real fix loses urgency.
- The proper fix in two days leaves half a day of slack for a change to code on the checkout path, the refund path, and the batch, inside the release marketing is watching. If the soak turns anything up Thursday, you're choosing between a rushed patch and pulling it that night. Not worth it for ten-minute flags.

The one thing that would change my call: if the flag volume is actually hurting support (I don't know the order volume), then the retry is a reasonable stopgap, but it ships as its own labeled change with a removal ticket, not as "the fix" and not bundled into Friday.
